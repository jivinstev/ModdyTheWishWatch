## Forks: please rename
If you publish a modified build, give it a different name and icon, and change the mod id and
display name in `gradle.properties` and `META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml`. That way players never
confuse your build with the official one, and bug reports reach the right people.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue in this repository.
